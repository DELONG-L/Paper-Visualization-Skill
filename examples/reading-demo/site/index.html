02 / The approach

Clarity with the research still intact.

A visual reading page should help the reader understand the work and return to its sources. The layout follows the paper’s argument, figures, and scope.

  1. 01

    The paper keeps its identity

    Retain the paper’s actual title and formal author names, so readers can identify the original work.

  2. 02

    Figures explain the argument

    Use the paper’s public figures where they help the reader understand a method, taxonomy, or result.

  3. 03

    The limits travel with the claim

    Preserve uncertainty and conditions. A short web explanation should stay within what the paper establishes.
